OCC Scholarship Overview

Hazel Park resident graduates are eligible to receive their first certificate or Associate’s Degree for free at Oakland Community College. This is a last-dollar scholarship, meaning students will draw from other funding sources before being awarded this scholarship.

All Hazel Park graduates attending Oakland Community College are eligible to receive up to $500 each semester for their mandatory books and supplies in their courses and programs regardless of high school graduation year. Eligible items for reimbursement include textbooks or books (physical or digital), software and technology, specialty protective or career apparel, and other school and studio supplies.

Once you complete the steps below, Oakland Community College will bill the Promise Zone directly. You can disregard the bill on your student account during the semester.

OCC Scholarship Eligibility and Requirements

Register with the Promise Zone

{% include svgs/checkbox.html class="fill-brand" %}

Live within the Hazel Park School District

{% include svgs/checkbox.html class="fill-brand" %}

Graduate from a Hazel Park School District high school within the past 6 years

{% include svgs/checkbox.html class="fill-brand" %}

Apply to Oakland Community College

{% include svgs/checkbox.html class="fill-brand" %}

Complete the Free Application for Federal Student Aid (FAFSA) every year

IMPORTANT NOTE: Select the Auburn Hills campus on your FAFSA regardless of which OCC campus you will be attending.

{% include svgs/checkbox.html class="fill-brand" %}

Complete the OCC Scholarship application (coming soon)

{% include svgs/checkbox.html class="fill-brand" %}

Enroll in at least 6 credits per semester

{% include svgs/checkbox.html class="fill-brand" %}

Buy necessary textbooks and school supplies

{% include svgs/checkbox.html class="fill-brand" %}

For in-person purchases, provide the cashier with your name, student ID number, and the name of our Promise Zone.

{% include svgs/checkbox.html class="fill-brand" %}

For online purchases, select “Financial Aid” at checkout and type in your student ID number followed by the word PROMISE (example: 7654321 PROMISE) to ensure the charges are applied to your Promise Zone account.

{% include svgs/checkbox.html class="fill-brand" %}

IMPORTANT NOTES: Be aware of the $500 per semester limit, going over could result in full or partial cancellation of your order. Track your purchases carefully. Students can return their purchases in accordance with the Bookstore’s Return Policy. Please contact us if there are any problems.

{% include svgs/checkbox.html class="fill-brand" %}

Maintain a 2.0 cumulative GPA and Satisfactory Academic Progress (SAP)
