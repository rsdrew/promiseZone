College Search and Choice

The simple act of looking at colleges can be daunting. With many different things to consider from size of the school to distance from home, finding even where to apply is sometimes difficult (but worth it!). Some of the resources below can help arm you with the tools to take on this task. If you are ever in doubt - reach out to your adviser, navigator, or the Promise Zone for help.

Financial Aid

There are many ways to get financial aid to help you pay for your education. Submitting the FAFSA should be your top priority, then getting scholarships and grants, then applying for student loans. The earlier you apply for any of these, the better!
