Postsecondary Success Coaching believes that:

Postsecondary education includes many pathways and should be accessible to all

College is any education beyond high school, including apprenticeship programs, licenses and certificates, Associate’s and Bachelor’s degrees, and professional and graduate programs.

Success depends on meaningful, ongoing support

Everyone is more likely to persist to graduation when they receive personalized, responsive guidance along the way.

The ability to further one’s education is a public good worth collective investment

Education strengthens individuals, communities, and our democracy.

Postsecondary Success Coaching includes:

Personalized Guidance

Reliable mentoring designed to help you succeed!

Academic Readiness

Build key academic habits and skills to stay focused through busy semesters.

Financial Guidance

Support in understanding financial aid and establishing effective budgeting and money habits.

Career Preparation

Exploring careers, finding opportunities, and preparing resumes and cover letters to land interviews.

Thoughtful Insight

Problem-solving advice when things get tough.
