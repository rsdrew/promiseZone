Viking Scholarship Overview

Hazel Park nonresident graduates are eligible to receive up to $500 per semester, up to a total of $2,000, across any two years of college enrollment. Scholarship funds can be applied to tuition, housing, associated fees, and other applicable educational expenses. This is a last-dollar scholarship, meaning students will draw from other funding sources before being awarded this scholarship.
