Supporting Vikings through their first year of college!

Our near-peer mentoring program pairs graduating high school seniors with enrolled college mentors to provide resources and encouragement through the first year of college.

Benefits of being a mentee:

Get guidance from real HPHS alumni

Build academic and life skills

Be accountable to someone just like you

Feel supported, confident, and connected

Ask questions, judgement free!

Benefits of being a mentor:

Develop leadership and career skills

Give back to the Hazel Park community

Be a role model and make an impact

Resumé- and network-builder
