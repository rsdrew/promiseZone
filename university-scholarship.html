University Scholarship Overview

Hazel Park resident graduates are eligible to receive up to $4,000 per semester for the first two years of college, and up to $1,000 per semester for their last two years of college. Scholarship funds can be applied to tuition, on-campus room and board, meal plans, associated fees, and other applicable educational expenses. This is a last-dollar scholarship, meaning students will draw from other funding sources before being awarded this scholarship.
