Why choose the Hazel Park Promise Zone?

The Hazel Park Promise Zone is a nonprofit organization committed to supporting the Hazel Park community by building a skilled, educated workforce and ensuring the city is an attractive destination for families. We do this by offering college and career planning and success services to ALL Hazel Park School District students, their families, and alumni. We are proud to be one of thirteen Promise Zones in Michigan.

Students face various challenges when preparing for college, including preparing for the SAT, finding and applying to the right college or program amid numerous options, and completing the complex Free Application for Federal Student Aid (FAFSA). Paying for a program and succeeding in it is difficult, too. Without personalized guidance, students often feel overwhelmed and uncertain about their paths after high school graduation.

The Hazel Park Promise Zone provides a holistic approach to address all of these challenges. Our dedicated team offers personalized assistance with college applications, completing the FAFSA, and career guidance. Through our diverse catalog of scholarships, one-on-one success coaching, and a near-peer mentorship program, Hazel Park School District alumni receive comprehensive support and resources to succeed in college and flourish in their professional pursuits. These efforts ensure a bright future for our students, their families, and our community.
