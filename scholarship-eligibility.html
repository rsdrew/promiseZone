Residential and College Eligibility

Our initial scholarship eligibility is based on where students live when they graduate from the Hazel Park School District. In addition, our scholarships are only available to students intending to further their education within the state of Michigan in accordance with the Promise Zone Authority Act of 2008. The only two exceptions to this are our Textbook and School Supplies Reimbursement and our Summer Scholarship. Our reimbursement funding is associated with a grant that does not require in-state college enrollment, and out-of-state students are welcome to use our Summer Scholarship for attending their in-district community college for summer courses in Michigan.
