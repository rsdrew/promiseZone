Thank you for attending the 2026 Race to College Dinner!

Since 2014, The Race to College Dinner reflects the full scope of the Promise Zone, bringing together students, families, alumni, administrators, partners, and sponsors to celebrate and fund our mission. It is where the state of the Promise is shared through both stories and our data-driven impact while awards recognize the people and partnerships that both believe in students, and do the work to support them. All funds raised during our Annual Fundraiser go directly back into the college access and success work we do at the Promise Zone.
